# Analysis Preregistration

## Research Question

To be defined after examining the available dataset variables, before examining outcome relationships.

## Hypotheses

The null and alternative hypotheses will be specified after the research question and variables have been defined.

## Population, Sample, and Exclusions

The target population, unit of analysis, inclusion criteria, exclusion criteria, and stopping rule will be specified based on the dataset and research question.

## Variables and Measures

The outcome, primary predictor, controls, transformations, and missing-data handling will be defined before the primary analysis.

## Analysis Plan

The statistical model, assumptions, significance level, effect size, confidence interval, and robustness checks will be specified before examining outcome patterns.

## Deviations

Any deviation from this preregistered plan will be recorded with a timestamp, reason, and description of the change.
